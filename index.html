Privacy Policy for Gullak

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Gullak ("the app") is developed by Zedex (the developer name shown on our Google Play Store listing). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you provide when you create an account. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text.
  • Savings data: the compartment names, goals, and amounts you enter. This data is yours — we do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • Savings history: the date, amount and (optional) source you record with each deposit or withdrawal, such as salary or a gift. This history is stored only on your device. It is not sent to our server and it is not shared with advertisers.
  • Purchase information: which in-app items you've unlocked, and a purchase record used to verify transactions with Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details — those are handled entirely by Google Play.

How we use this information

Solely to operate the app: to sign you in, to sync your savings and purchases across devices, and to verify purchases with Google Play. We do not sell your account or savings data, and the amounts, goals, and compartment names you enter are never shared with advertisers. The free app shows ads provided by Google AdMob — see "Advertising" below.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all data associated with it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • On the web: https://gullak-backend-id78.onrender.com/delete-account
  • By email: if you cannot sign in, write to zedexapps@gmail.com from the address you registered with.

Deleting your account permanently removes your email address, password, purchase and unlock records, and any savings data synced to our server. It cannot be undone. Savings kept only on your phone are not held by us; you can erase them with Settings → Reset entire app or by uninstalling. Google Play keeps its own record of payments you made, which we cannot delete.

Advertising

Gullak shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner on the Insights screen, an occasional full-screen ad when you return to the main screen, and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to try a premium theme free for a day. To load and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect and use information such as your IP address, your device's advertising ID, device and app diagnostics, and how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK, ads are requested only after you make a choice in a consent form, and you can change that choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Play Billing to process purchases and Google AdMob for the optional rewarded ads. These services have their own privacy policies governing the data they process.

Age restriction

Gullak is intended for users 18 and older, consistent with the target audience we've declared for this app on Google Play. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact
