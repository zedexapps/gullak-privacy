Privacy Policy for Gullak

Last updated: October 2026 (version 1.5 of the app)

Gullak ("the app") is developed by Zedex Apps (the developer name shown on our Google Play Store listing). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

In short: the savings you record stay on your phone. They leave it only if you choose to make a backup in your Gullak account. We do not sell your data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which you can control. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Information we collect

  • Account information (optional): the email address and password you provide when you create an account. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text. You can use the app without an account.
  • Savings data and history: the compartment names, goals, amounts, dates and (optional) sources, such as salary or a gift, that you record, plus your savings challenges and streaks. This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to our server and it is not shared with advertisers — unless you choose to make a cloud backup (next point).
  • Cloud backup (optional): if you are signed in and tap "Back up now", or switch on automatic backup, a copy of your compartments, balances, history, goals, savings challenges and a few settings (currency, language, reminder time, "hide amounts") is stored on our server and linked to your account, so you can restore it on a new phone. It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It is not end-to-end encrypted. We use it for nothing else. You can delete it at any time (see "Data retention and deletion").
  • Purchase information: which in-app items you've unlocked, and a purchase record (the item, the Google Play order ID and purchase token, and the time) used to verify transactions with Google Play and to restore your purchases. We do not receive or store your card details — those are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Password reset: if you tap "Forgot password?", we send a one-time 6-digit code to your email address through an email delivery service. The code is stored only as a hash and expires after 15 minutes.
  • Error reports: when the app has an error, it may send us the error message, a short technical trace, the app version, the name of the screen and a description of your browser/device. These reports are used only to find and fix bugs. They are not meant to contain your name, email address or any amounts, and we do not add your savings data to them.

We do not collect your contacts, messages, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The optional app lock PIN is stored only on your phone, as a salted hash. The optional daily reminder is a notification created on your phone; we do not send push notifications.

How we use this information

Solely to operate and improve the app: to sign you in, to verify and restore purchases with Google Play, to let you back up and restore your savings if you choose to, to reset a forgotten password, and to find and fix errors. We do not sell your account or savings data, and the amounts, goals, and compartment names you enter are never shared with advertisers. The free app shows ads provided by Google AdMob — see "Advertising" below.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all data associated with it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • On the web: https://gullak-backend-id78.onrender.com/delete-account
  • By email: if you cannot sign in, first try "Forgot password?" in the app. If that does not work, write to zedexapps@gmail.com from the address you registered with.

Delete only your cloud backup (and keep your account): in the app, Settings → Backup & export → "Delete my cloud backup". Your savings on your phone are not affected.

Deleting your account permanently removes your email address, password, purchase and unlock records, your cloud backup and any pending password-reset code from our database. It cannot be undone. Our server keeps daily safety copies of its database for up to 7 days; deleted data disappears from them when they are replaced. Our hosting provider keeps technical logs (such as IP addresses) for a limited time under its own policy. Error reports contain no account details and only the most recent few hundred are kept. Savings kept only on your phone are not held by us; you can erase them with Settings → Reset entire app or by uninstalling. Google Play keeps its own record of payments you made, which we cannot delete.

Advertising

Gullak shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner on the Insights screen, an occasional full-screen ad when you return to the main screen, and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to try a premium theme free for a day. The "Remove Ads" and "Everything Bundle" purchases turn the ads off. To load and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect and use information such as your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), your device's advertising ID, device and app diagnostics, and how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK, ads are requested only after you make a choice in a consent form, and you can change that choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Third-party services

The app and its server use the following services. They have their own privacy policies governing the data they process.

  • Google Play (Billing, and the Google Play Developer API on our server) to process and verify purchases.
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent message for ads, including the optional rewarded ads.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) to display the app's fonts. Google receives your IP address and device information when it does.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs, only if the app's built-in 3D library file cannot be loaded. In that case it is downloaded from there and Cloudflare receives your IP address.
  • Render (cloud hosting) runs our server and database.
  • Brevo sends the password-reset email (your email address and the one-time code).

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. We do not sell your information.

Security

Information sent between the app and our server travels over HTTPS. Passwords and reset codes are stored hashed, and repeated wrong password attempts are slowed down. The app-lock PIN keeps casual onlookers out of the app but does not encrypt the data stored on your phone, so please also use your phone's screen lock. No system is perfectly secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or the California CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to receive a copy, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. In the app you can export your history, delete your backup or your account, and change your ad choices. For anything else, email zedexapps@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Age restriction

Gullak is intended for users 18 and older, consistent with the target audience we've declared for this app on Google Play. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
